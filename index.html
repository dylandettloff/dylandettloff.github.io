A Python-based multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA) framework for comparing water technologies across technical and environmental conditions.

PythonData pipelinesMCDAScenario analysis

The question

How do technology choices change when deployment conditions and priorities change?

The implementation

Four technologies, five community contexts, and five futures. A salinity screen, 11 scoring criteria, and Monte Carlo simulation turn assumptions into comparable rankings.
